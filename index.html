Getting around

No car needed. The Airporter drops you at your Banff hotel, and the Lake Louise ski bus picks up at Banff hotels for free.

Banff Airporter · YYC → Banff

CA$88.90 one way, CA$177.80 return + GST. About 2 hours. Winter schedule from Dec 15.

Last bus leaves at 11:00 PM, before we clear customs on Dec 31. Plan on 9:00 AM Jan 1.

Banff Airporter · Banff → YYC

Arrives 2 hours after departure.

The first bus arrives at 7:00 AM, too late for a 7 AM flight, so we sleep at the airport the night before.

Free ski bus · Banff ⇄ Lake Louise

Picks up at Banff hotels including Banff Caribou Lodge, Banff Park Lodge and the Brewster Transportation Centre. About an hour each way.

Super 8 Calgary Airport

3030 Barlow Trail NE, about 5 km from the terminal. Airport shuttle needs a reservation and may cost about CA$10 per room. Free breakfast.

Book the shuttle for Dec 31 late night and for the last night before a 7 AM flight.

Where to stay in Banff
